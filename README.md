  # Digital Patwari

Digital Patwari is an evidence-first system for understanding Punjab Fard and Jamabandi land records.

## Purpose

Digital Patwari aims to help citizens upload a Fard and clearly understand:

- Owners and their recorded shares
- Khewats and Khataunis
- Khasra numbers and land areas
- Mutations and ownership changes
- Possession and cultivation entries
- Mortgages, remarks and other encumbrances
- Calculations, inconsistencies and uncertain readings
- Evidence from the original record for every important answer

## How it works

1. Upload a Punjab Fard or Jamabandi.
2. Each page is read and analysed separately.
3. Related Khewats, Khataunis and Khasras are joined across pages.
4. Ownership shares and land areas are calculated.
5. Important findings are linked to evidence from the source document.
6. Unclear or conflicting entries are shown for human verification.

## Current status

Digital Patwari is under active development.

The current pipeline supports multi-page Punjab land records, per-page extraction, cross-page merging, rate-limit handling, retries and partial-failure reporting.

## Project principles

- Evidence before conclusions
- Uncertainty must be visible
- No silent assumptions
- Simple citizen-facing explanations
- Punjabi and English support
- Privacy by design

## Important notice

Digital Patwari explains information contained in land records. It does not make a legal determination of ownership and does not replace verification by the Punjab Revenue Department or advice from a qualified legal professional.
