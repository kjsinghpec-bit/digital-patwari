Multi-Khatauni protection

✓ Correct total (what Digital Patwari reports)
8 Kanal 10 Marla
Each Khatauni's own recorded area counted exactly once: 5-0-0 (KHT-1) + 3-10-0 (KHT-2).
✗ Naive incorrect result avoided
17 Kanal 0 Marla
What a buggy system would show if it multiplied the whole Khewat area by its 2 Khataunis instead of summing them once.
Digital Patwari's validation layer (AG-03) checks this down to the Sarsahi for every Khatauni in a Khewat — a Khewat's area is never inferred by multiplying, only by summing each Khatauni's own recorded area exactly once.
